FORT

A space to build, change and inhabit together.

Built

The question

How can the familiar act of building a blanket fort invite people to make a space together?

My contribution

Concept, spatial design, physical prototyping, fabrication and installation.

The result

A shared space built and changed collectively by visitors throughout Culture Night.

FORT was a participatory installation for Culture Night, hosted by MTU Gallery at 46 Grand Parade, Cork, and Creativity & Change. It was built around one of our earliest experiences of making a space: blanket and box forts.

Using sheets, clips, pegs, cardboard, art supplies and a simple wooden framework, visitors were invited to change and inhabit the space together throughout the evening. The structure was a starting point, with people deciding what it could become.

Before we ever learn about architecture or design, many of us have already experimented with space in this way. FORT was an invitation to enter that creative state again, without the usual concerns about cost, noise or regulations.

What started as an empty structure gradually became something made collectively: messy, playful, collaborative, weird and fun, with The Prodigy playing in the background. People of all ages understood what to do with very little instruction. Volunteers, MTU staff, friends and family helped bring it all together.
