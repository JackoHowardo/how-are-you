02 / Public + community

Make room for something to happen.

I work with public spaces, temporary structures and participatory processes to create opportunities for gathering, use and connection.

Who this is for

Community groups, cultural organisations, local authorities and project partners working with a place, a public question or a group of people.

Ways we could work

The format follows the place and the people involved, from early research to a tested spatial intervention.

01

Public-space interventions

Small, strategic changes that support comfort, access, meeting and everyday use.

02

Temporary architecture

Structures for shelter, performance, conversation, gathering and shared activity.

03

Activation

Events, programming and spatial prompts that help an overlooked place become inhabited.

04

Facilitation + co-design

Workshops and accessible processes that let lived experience shape the brief and response.

A situated process

Observe

Spend time in place: watching movement, use, weather, edges, absences and informal adaptations.

Listen

Bring local knowledge and lived experience into the project before deciding what the answer is.

Test

Use drawings, models, temporary objects and small experiments to make ideas discussable.

Activate

Develop a useful intervention and a plan for how people can inhabit, adapt and sustain it.
