02 / Art + collaboration

A shared question.
Room to explore.

Start a conversation ↗Find your starting point ↓

I develop artistic and spatial projects with communities, artists and organisations, exploring how we gather, inhabit places and relate to living systems. The work grows through shared enquiry, participation and experimentation.

A starting point

Bring a place, a question or a shared interest. We can explore the idea, the partnership and how the work could be supported.

Who I work with

Find a starting point below. Every project takes its own shape.

01

Communities + local groups

Neighbourhood groups, community organisations and people connected by a place or shared experience.+

Where I can help

Explore a question together and give local knowledge a creative form.

What I can offer

Collaborative research, workshops, spatial experiments and participatory installations.

What it could look like

A project exploring how an overlooked local space could support gathering, beginning with stories and shared activities and developing a temporary intervention together.

An illustrative starting point
02

Artists + cultural partners

Artists, curators, galleries, festivals and cultural organisations.+

Where I can help

Develop work where space, participation and artistic enquiry meet.

What I can offer

Spatial concepts, collaborative making, installation design and creative facilitation.

What it could look like

An artist and a community group developing an installation that visitors can inhabit and contribute to, with the form emerging through research and experimentation.

An illustrative starting point
03

Environmental + research partners

Environmental groups, researchers and organisations working with landscapes and living systems.+

Where I can help

Explore how people relate to changing environments through creative practice.

What I can offer

Place-based enquiry, fieldwork, participatory activities and spatial responses.

What it could look like

A collaborative enquiry into a changing landscape, bringing together local stories, observation and material experiments to ask how people might relate to it differently.

An illustrative starting point
04

Project + funding partners

Organisations and practitioners developing joint proposals, exchanges or residencies.+

Where I can help

Turn a shared interest into a project with a clear purpose and a workable structure.

What I can offer

Concept development, research methods, workshop planning and a spatial design contribution to a joint proposal.

What it could look like

Developing a funded exchange around a shared place-based question, with agreed roles, a research programme and time for communities to shape the direction.

An illustrative starting point

What I bring

Different approaches, brought together around the purpose of your project.

01

Spatial practice

Explore how an idea takes shape through space: installations, temporary structures, gathering places and interventions in everyday environments.

02

Participation + co-creation

Create ways for people to contribute their experiences and develop the direction of a project together.

03

Place-based research

Use observation, conversations, walks and material experiments to explore the relationships between people, places and living systems.

04

Project development

Shape a shared concept, agree roles and methods, and develop a realistic plan and budget for a funding proposal or supported collaboration.

How we get started

Find common ground

Talk about the place, question or interest that brings us together.

Shape the enquiry

Agree who should be involved and how they can influence the work.

Plan + resource

Develop roles, a budget including practitioner fees, and a suitable funding or commissioning route.

Explore together

Begin the agreed research and creative process, with room to respond to what emerges.
